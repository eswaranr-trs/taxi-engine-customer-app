Home Screen — 5 Designs

Five different layouts for home_screen.dart + home_content.dart. Each design keeps everything the current home screen does. Two states are shown per design.

Everything the current home screen has (all 5 designs keep these):
  • Trip type: One Way · Round Trip · Airport Transfer
  • Pickup (defaults to Current Location) and Destination
  • Add up to 3 stops, with a Manage option
  • Airport mode: To Airport / From Airport + airport picker
  • Notifications bell with unread badge
  • Ongoing trip button (shown when a trip is active)
  • Map with nearby driver markers + "set on map" pin mode
  • Bottom nav: Home · Bookings · Deals · Profile
Map stays full screen

1 · Floating Glass

Closest to today's layout, cleaned up. Full map underneath; one frosted card holds the trip type and the route. Maroon to match the new login screen.

  • Trip type: segmented control at the top of the card.
  • Route: pickup + destination rows joined by a dashed line; + button adds a stop; stops collapse into one "2 stops · Manage" row.
  • Notifications: top-right, next to a location chip.
  • Ongoing trip: maroon bar above the nav with driver ETA.
  • Nav: floating pill; active tab shows its label.

2 · Bottom Sheet

Map on top, controls in a sheet at the bottom where the thumb already is. Black and white with gold highlights; feels calm and serious.

  • Trip type: underlined tabs at the top of the sheet.
  • Route: pickup is a small "From" line (tap Change); the big "Where to?" box is the destination. Stops are chips under it.
  • Extra: recent places so repeat trips are one tap.
  • Ongoing trip: black card floating on the map with plate number.
  • Nav: classic flat bar with a gold marker on the active tab.

3 · Dashboard Header

No full-screen map. A coloured header with a greeting, big trip-type tiles, the route card, then a small map preview. Friendly teal.

  • Trip type: three large tiles overlapping the header; easy to tap.
  • Route: card with pickup, stops and destination listed in order, a swap button, and a dashed "Add stop" row.
  • Notifications: in the header next to the greeting.
  • Ongoing trip: dark card with a progress bar.
  • Map: small preview showing drivers nearby; tap to open full map.

4 · Night Command Bar

Dark map, and every control sits in one panel at the bottom. The ongoing trip lives in a small "live" pill at the top, like the iPhone Dynamic Island.

  • Trip type: compact chips at the top of the panel.
  • Route: large FROM / TO text with a round swap button; stops as chips below.
  • Notifications + my location: small button column on the right of the map.
  • Ongoing trip: live pill at the top; tap to open trip details.
  • Nav: icon-only bar; active tab marked with a dot.

5 · Text-First Split

Top half is a simple form with a big question; the bottom half is a map peek. Uses the app's existing orange so it still feels like the same product.

  • Route: two separate input boxes; the active one is outlined in orange with a + for stops.
  • Map: peeks up from the bottom with drivers; "Set on map" opens pin mode.
  • Ongoing trip: orange pill on the map (same as today).
