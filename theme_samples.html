Taxi Customer App — 3 UX Styles

Three different ways the app could work, not just three color schemes. Each one changes the layout, navigation and booking flow. The same three screens (Login, Home, Choose Ride) are shown for each so you can compare.

Like Uber · Bolt

A · Map-First

The map is the app. Everything happens in a bottom sheet that slides over the map. Warm maroon look with the sunset illustration.

  • Navigation: side drawer (☰). No tab bar, so the map gets the full screen.
  • Booking: type where to go → choose a ride in the same sheet → confirm. 2 taps.
  • Best for: a ride-only app where people book fast and see cars nearby.
  • Weak spot: little room for offers, rentals or other services.

Like Ola · Rapido · Grab

B · Super-App Dashboard

Home is a dashboard of cards, not a map. Services, offers and recent trips sit on the first screen, with a tab bar at the bottom. Clean teal look.

  • Navigation: bottom tab bar (Home, Trips, Wallet, Profile).
  • Booking: pick a service tile or enter where to go → full-page ride options with fare breakdown and coupon.
  • Best for: several services (Auto, Rental, Outstation, Parcel), promotions and a wallet.
  • Weak spot: one extra step before you see the map and cars.

Bold & minimal

C · One Step at a Time

Large text, one question per screen, very little on screen. Dark with gold, so it feels premium and is easy on the eyes at night.

  • Navigation: small floating pill bar at the bottom.
  • Booking: "Where to?" → swipe through cars one at a time → slide to book (no accidental taps).
  • Best for: a premium or airport-cab brand that wants to stand out.
  • Weak spot: comparing prices takes swipes instead of a glance.
